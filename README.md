# Jyoti Prakash Sahoo


## About Me

I am an academic and researcher working at the intersection of **Quantum AI, Federated Learning, and Cybersecurity**. My research focuses on quantum–classical machine learning, explainable and privacy-preserving AI, and secure intelligent systems for Edge Computing and Industrial IoT.

My broader research interests include Generative AI for cybersecurity, trustworthy machine learning, and distributed intelligence.
